ZKProofport — Brand kit
Version: 2026-10-13

IDENTITY
Write ZKProofport as one word. Only ZKP uses the pixel treatment; roofport
uses clean Inter outlines. The nine-row grid contains 114 tiles, reduced
from 202 in the original concept. Keep all tiles, spacing and colors intact.
The Z uses an optically corrected seven-column grid with a symmetrical
diagonal, matching the tile size and cap height of K and P.
The shield and anchor are the unchanged original product symbol.

FILES
logo-light / logo-dark: horizontal shield and wordmark for light / dark backgrounds.
stacked-light / stacked-dark: centered vertical versions.
wordmark-light / wordmark-dark: gold ZKP with navy / paper suffix.
wordmark-mono-light / wordmark-mono-dark: single-color wordmarks.
symbol: unchanged shield and anchor, original 600 x 600 PNG.
cover: 1600 x 900 brand cover.
SVG wordmarks are outlined vectors and require no font installation.
SVG lockups embed the original 600 px shield bitmap. Use the wordmark-only
SVG for fully vector output at very large sizes. PNG logos are transparent;
the cover is opaque. The shield colors stay intact in every version.

COLORS
Midnight #071827; Gold #CBA65E; Light Gold #DFC18A; Paper #F2F3EE.
The pixel pattern also uses a darker gold #BA954F.
TYPOGRAPHY
Inter: 750 for the source ZKP glyphs, 650 for roofport.
Font license: INTER-OFL.txt (SIL Open Font License).

USAGE
Leave clear space of at least half the capital-letter height around the mark.
Use the horizontal logo at 240 px wide or larger; wordmark alone at 160 px
or larger. For tighter placements use the shield alone, at least 32 px tall.
Choose light or dark variants for contrast. Do not rotate, stretch, recolor,
remove tiles, add shadows, or pixelate the shield. Do not imply endorsement
or affiliation without permission.
